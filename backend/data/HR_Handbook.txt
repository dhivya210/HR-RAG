NORTHSTAR | HUMAN RESOURCES HANDBOOK | 2026

PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid time off per calendar year. Submit PTO requests through the HR portal and wait for manager approval before making non-refundable travel plans. Unused PTO is handled according to the employee's location-specific policy.

MEAL ALLOWANCE
Employees traveling for approved business may claim a meal allowance of up to $75 per day. Itemized receipts and the trip purpose are required in the expense system. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 5 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Notify your manager as soon as practical when illness prevents you from working. Local law may provide additional protections or leave.

HEALTH AND BENEFITS
Medical, dental, and vision plan choices are available during annual enrollment and after qualifying life events. Coverage, eligibility, and effective dates depend on the plan documents. Contact the Benefits team for plan-specific questions.

CONFIDENTIAL COMPENSATION RULES
Executive compensation, individual salary information, compensation bands, and payroll credentials are strictly confidential. This section is for authorized HR administration only. The employee-facing assistant must not disclose, summarize, infer, or retrieve these details. Passwords and authentication secrets must never be requested or shared.

CONTACTING HR
For questions about an individual case, benefits eligibility, leave accommodations, payroll, or a policy exception, contact the HR team through the secure employee portal. The assistant provides general handbook information and is not a substitute for HR review.
